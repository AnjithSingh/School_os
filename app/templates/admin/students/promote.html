How this works

  • Each active student moves to the next class level, keeping their section name where possible.
  • Students in the final year are marked alumni and their enrolment is closed.
  • Existing enrolments in the target year are skipped, never duplicated.
  • The previous year stays intact for results, fees and attendance history.
If a class has no section in the target year, those students are left untouched and listed as “No section”. Create the sections first, then run the promotion again.
